HOW IT WORKS

One trip for all of you, in six steps

A place you can all fly to, on the same dates, with the full price for everyone — cheapest first.

  1. Add everyone's airportTwo to eight departures: yours, your partner's, your friends' or your family's. Several people leaving from the same place? Press + on that airport instead of adding it twice.
  2. Choose Anywhere, or a place you have in mindLeave “Going to” on Anywhere and we look for destinations everyone can reach. Already know where you're going? Pick it, and we compare the coming weekends for you instead.
  3. Say whenA weekend, a long weekend, a full week, a number of nights you choose, or exact dates.
  4. See the cheapest total firstEvery result is one trip for all of you: dates that work for everyone, direct flights or one stop, and everyone's flights added up — the real price of meeting, not a price per person.
  5. Land at about the same time“Everyone lands within 3 h” makes sure nobody waits alone at the airport for hours. On a weekend, the flights home are close together too, so nobody loses a day — and we never go past 4 hours. On longer trips, one of you may arrive or leave a day apart when that finds you a trip; we always show it. You can change the hours with − and +.
  6. Book directly on AviasalesPress Select to see your trip, then each of you books your own flight, from your own airport, on Aviasales — at the price shown there. For a “good place”, press Choose dates, pick a week that suits you all, and each of you opens the live prices.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no fee and no account to create.

How does Halfway Trips make money?

The “Book on Aviasales” buttons are affiliate links (through Travelpayouts). If you book through one, we may earn a small commission from Aviasales. You pay the same price either way.

Are the prices live?

They come from recent searches made by travellers, so the live price on Aviasales can be a little different, and some routes or dates may be missing. Always check the final price before you book.

What is a “good place”?

A place where each of you has a cheap flight, just not on the same dates in the prices we have yet. We show each of you the lowest price we've seen and the total together. Choose dates that suit you all, and Aviasales shows you the live prices for exactly those dates.

Why is there a “≈” in front of arrival times?

The flight data we use has departure times only. Arrival times and flight lengths are worked out from the distance and the time zones, so they are estimates.

Do you sell the tickets?

No. We help you find the trip; you book it with Aviasales and the airline or agency they show you. Changes, cancellations and refunds are handled by them.

Can I find hotels too?

Not yet — places to stay for your dates are coming to the trip page.
