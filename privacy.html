PRIVACY

Your privacy

No cookies, no account needed, nothing that identifies you — unless you choose to create an account. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • We don't use cookies, and you don't need an account. If you create one, we keep only your email address, the trips you save and your 5 latest searches — and you can delete it all at once.
  • We count visits and clicks anonymously, to see what works and what to improve.
  • Your search (airports, dates, number of travellers) is sent to our flight-data partner to find prices.
  • When you book, you leave our site for Aviasales, and their privacy policy applies.

Who we are

Halfway Trips (halfwaytrips.com) is run by Maxime, in Stockholm, Sweden — “we” on this page. For anything about your data, write to hello@halfwaytrips.com.

When you search

The airports, destination, dates, number of travellers and currency you choose are sent to our search service (run on Supabase), which asks Travelpayouts — our flight-data and affiliate partner — for prices. We keep the prices we receive for a while, so later searches are faster; we don't keep who searched for them.

While you type the name of an airport or city, the letters you type are sent to Travelpayouts' public airport lookup, to suggest matching airports.

If you create an account

An account is optional. To sign in, you give your email address and we send you a sign-in link and code — there is no password. Signing in and our database are run by Supabase, which keeps standard sign-in logs (such as the time and the IP address) for security. The sign-in emails are sent by Resend, from its servers in the EU (Ireland); it keeps a record of each email it sends (the address and the time) for a limited period, to deliver them and to prevent abuse.

With an account we keep your email address, the trips you save (the place, the dates, the airports and the price when you saved it) and your 5 latest searches (the airports, the destination and the dates you chose), so they are ready on any device where you sign in. We use them only for that: no newsletter, and we don't share or sell them. We do this to provide the account you asked for.

They stay until you remove them or delete your account. On the Account page you can remove a saved trip, forget your latest searches, or delete your account and everything in it, straight away. (The technical logs above are deleted on their own after a while.)

Visitor statistics

We use Umami Cloud, a privacy-friendly analytics service that doesn't use cookies. It records the pages you visit, the website you came from, your browser, operating system and type of device, and your approximate location (country and region), which is worked out from your IP address — your IP address itself is not stored.

It also records what you do on the site: that a search was made (the airport codes, the kind of search, the number of travellers and how many results were found), that you opened a trip or a good place (or pressed “Select” or “Choose dates”), and that you pressed “Book on Aviasales” or “See live prices on Aviasales”. We only look at these numbers together, never to follow one person. This is in our legitimate interest of understanding and improving the site.

When you press “Book on Aviasales”

We turn your trip (airports, dates, number of seats) into a partner link through Travelpayouts, and your browser is sent through a Travelpayouts address to Aviasales. From then on, Travelpayouts and Aviasales may use cookies — for example to know that the booking came from us, so that we can earn a commission. Their own privacy policies apply to everything you do on their websites, including your booking.

Services that help show the page

Like most websites, ours loads some files from other services, and each of them receives your IP address when your browser fetches those files:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the website;
  • jsDelivr, for the world map and the tools that draw it;
  • Umami, for the visitor statistics above;
  • Supabase and Travelpayouts, for searches and booking links.

These providers may keep standard technical logs (such as the time of a request and the IP address) for security and to run their service.

Cookies

Halfway Trips doesn't set any cookies. If you sign in, your browser keeps your sign-in (a token, in the browser's storage — not a cookie) until you sign out, and while you sign in it keeps the email you typed, for that tab only. If you choose a currency, your browser remembers it for next time (also in its storage, never sent to us). Otherwise we store nothing in your browser. Cookies may be set by Aviasales and Travelpayouts once you follow a booking link to their websites.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Without an account we don't keep data that is linked to you, so there is usually nothing to find; with one, the Account page shows everything we keep — but write to hello@halfwaytrips.com and we'll answer. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Sweden, where we are based, that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se), or you can turn to the one in your own country.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes — for example when we add hotels — we will update this page and the date at the top.
