PRIVACY

Your privacy

No cookies, no account, nothing that identifies you. Here is exactly what happens with your data.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version

  • We don't use cookies, and we don't ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • We count visits and clicks anonymously, to see what works and what to improve.
  • Your search (airports, dates, number of travellers) is sent to our flight-data partner to find prices.
  • When you book, you leave our site for Aviasales, and their privacy policy applies.

Who we are

Halfway Trips (halfwaytrips.com) is run by Maxime, in Stockholm, Sweden — “we” on this page. For anything about your data, write to hello@halfwaytrips.com.

When you search

The airports, destination, dates, number of travellers and currency you choose are sent to our search service (run on Supabase), which asks Travelpayouts — our flight-data and affiliate partner — for prices. We keep the prices we receive for a while, so later searches are faster; we don't keep who searched for them.

While you type the name of an airport or city, the letters you type are sent to Travelpayouts' public airport lookup, to suggest matching airports.

Visitor statistics

We use Umami Cloud, a privacy-friendly analytics service that doesn't use cookies. It records the pages you visit, the website you came from, your browser, operating system and type of device, and your approximate location (country and region), which is worked out from your IP address — your IP address itself is not stored.

It also records what you do on the site: that a search was made (the airport codes, the kind of search, the number of travellers and how many results were found), that you pressed “Select” or “Choose dates”, and that you pressed “Book on Aviasales” or “See live prices on Aviasales”. We only look at these numbers together, never to follow one person. This is in our legitimate interest of understanding and improving the site.

When you press “Book on Aviasales”

We turn your trip (airports, dates, number of seats) into a partner link through Travelpayouts, and your browser is sent through a Travelpayouts address to Aviasales. From then on, Travelpayouts and Aviasales may use cookies — for example to know that the booking came from us, so that we can earn a commission. Their own privacy policies apply to everything you do on their websites, including your booking.

Services that help show the page

Like most websites, ours loads some files from other services, and each of them receives your IP address when your browser fetches those files:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the website;
  • Google Fonts (Google), for the fonts;
  • jsDelivr, for the world map and the tools that draw it;
  • Umami, for the visitor statistics above;
  • Supabase and Travelpayouts, for searches and booking links.

These providers may keep standard technical logs (such as the time of a request and the IP address) for security and to run their service.

Cookies

Halfway Trips doesn't set any cookies and doesn't store anything in your browser. Cookies may be set by Aviasales and Travelpayouts once you follow a booking link to their websites.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Since we don't keep data that is linked to you, there is usually nothing to find — but write to hello@halfwaytrips.com and we'll answer. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Sweden, where we are based, that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se), or you can turn to the one in your own country.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes — for example when we add hotels or accounts — we will update this page and the date at the top.
