Made for people who live apart

For long-distance love, friends and family.

Halfway Trips helps people who live in different cities — or different countries — find a place to meet, on dates that work for all of them, for the lowest total price.

How it started

Hi, I'm Maxime. I live in Stockholm, and for more than a year my girlfriend has lived in Belgium. Seeing each other means flying, so we decided to make every trip count: we meet in European capitals that neither of us knows yet.

Flying that often adds up, so we always look for cheap flights — and that was the hard part. First we'd find a weekend with a good price from Stockholm to one city, then check the same dates from Brussels, where the price was often much higher. So we'd start again with another city, and another.

Our evenings on the phone, the little time we have together, went into comparing flights instead of talking. We looked for a site that would do it for us, and it didn't exist. So I built it.

What it does

Halfway Trips searches from both airports — or everyone's, for a group — at once, keeps only the dates that work for all of you, and shows the full price for everyone, cheapest first. All the options are in front of you, and the only question left is the fun one: where do we want to go?

What we care about

  • The real price. Everyone's flights added up, not a price per person.
  • Time together. Dates that work for everyone, and arrivals close together, so nobody waits alone at the airport.
  • Honesty. We say when a price or a time is an estimate, and you always book directly with Aviasales.
  • Simplicity. No account and no fees.
