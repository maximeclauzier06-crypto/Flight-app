ABOUT US

Made for people who live apart

For long-distance love, friends and family.

Halfway Trips helps people who live in different cities — or different countries — find a place to meet, on dates that work for all of them, for the lowest total price.

WHERE IT STARTED

Far apart, and always searching

Hi, I'm Maxime. I live in Stockholm, and for more than a year my girlfriend has lived in Belgium. Seeing each other means flying, so we decided to make every trip count: we meet in European capitals that neither of us knows yet.

Finding the flights was the hard part. A good weekend from Stockholm was often expensive from Brussels, so we started again with another city, and another — and our evenings on the phone went into comparing flights instead of talking.

THE IDEA

One search, from both airports

Flight search sites start from one city. We wanted the opposite: both our airports at once, the same dates for both of us, and both prices added up — every place we could meet, cheapest first. We looked for it, and it didn't exist.

THE START

So I built it

I started from one simple question — where can we both fly, on the same weekend, for the least money? — and built Halfway Trips piece by piece to answer it.

What keeps me going: every trip we plan this way leaves more of our time for each other. I'd like that for everyone who lives apart from someone they love.

WHAT IT DOES

Every place you can meet, in one list

Add your airport and theirs — or everyone's, for a group. Halfway Trips keeps only the dates that work for all of you, adds up everyone's flights, and shows the cheapest first. Then each of you books your own flight on Aviasales.

WHY WE DO IT

Spend it on the trip, not the search

Couples who live apart, friends in different countries, families spread across a continent: meeting up shouldn't start with hours of searching, or with paying far more than you need to. We want to make that part easy — all the options in front of you, and only the fun question left: where do we go?

What we care about

The real priceEveryone's flights added up, not a price per person.
Time togetherDates that work for everyone, and arrivals close together, so nobody waits alone at the airport.
HonestyWe say when a price or a time is an estimate, and you always book directly with Aviasales.
